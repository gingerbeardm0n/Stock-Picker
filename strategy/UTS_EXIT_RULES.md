## The Exit Philosophy

**Core Rule**: Sell in LAYERS, not all at once.

**Base Hit Targets**: 15-25 cents per share = $150-$250 on 1,000 shares

**Scaling Out Strategy**: Take profits at levels, move stop to breakeven on remainder, let the rest run.

## Profit-Taking Rules (Scaling Out)

### ✅ **Standard 2-Level Scaling** (Recommended for beginners)

**Sell 50% at First Target**:
- Target = 2:1 Risk/Reward or retest of day's high
- Example: Entry $6.50, stop $6.40 (10¢ risk) → first target $6.70 (20¢ = 2:1)
- Sell 500 shares at $6.70, lock in $100 profit
- Remaining 500 shares = "house money" trade (risk-free)

**Move Stop to Breakeven on Remaining 50%**:
- Stop loss on remaining 500 shares = move to entry ($6.50)
- Now worst case = breakeven, best case = unlimited upside on remaining position
- This creates asymmetrical risk (no loss on remainder, all upside)

**Let Remaining 50% Run to 3:1 or Next Resistance**:
- 3:1 R/R on original 10¢ stop = $6.80 (30¢ gain)
- Or hold until:
  - Hit resistance (prior daily high)
  - Volume dries up (sell signal)
  - Candle closes below 9 EMA (reversal signal)
  - Time decay (approaching 11 AM, less probable)

**Example**:
```
Entry: 1,000 shares @ $6.50
Stop: $6.40 (10¢ risk max)
First Target: $6.70 (20¢ = 2:1 R/R)

ACTION AT $6.70:
  - Sell 500 shares → lock in $100 profit
  - Move stop on remaining 500 to $6.50 (entry, breakeven)
  - Set target for remainder: $6.80-$7.00 (3:1 or resistance)

IF REMAINDER HITS $6.80:
  - Sell 250 more (take another $150)
  - Trailing stop on final 250 (move stop up as price rises)

IF REMAINDER HITS $7.00:
  - Sell remaining 250 → lock in $250 on second batch
  - Total profit = $100 + $150 + $250 = $500 on $6,500 entry
```

### ⚠️ **Advanced 3-Level Scaling** (For experienced traders)

**25% at First Target (2:1 R/R)**:
- Lock in initial gains
- Example: 1,000 shares, sell 250 at $6.70 → $50 locked

**25% at Second Target (3:1 R/R)**:
- Sell another 250 at $6.80 → $100 locked (on original shares)
- Remaining 500 shares = pure house money

**50% Trailing Stop**:
- Let remaining 500 ride with trailing 5-cent stop
- Hold until:
  - Stop hit
  - Candle closes below 9 EMA
  - Volume dries up
  - Time decay (approaching noon)

## Exit Signals (When to Cut & Run)

### 🛑 **Hard Stops — MUST Exit Immediately**

**1. Price Hits Your Stop Loss**
- Non-negotiable. Exit ALL shares.
- This is tuition. You paid it — move on.
- If you move stop "just 2 more cents" you lose.

**2. Daily Max Loss Hit**
- If max daily loss ($200-$300) hit on first 1-2 trades → STOP TRADING FOR THE DAY
- Walk away. Don't revenge trade.
- Your edge returns tomorrow.

**3. Account Turns Red (Green-to-Red Rule)**
- Started day up $200 → now down $50 → STOP TRADING
- Take the rest of the day off
- Psychology matters: don't try to recover losses same day

**4. Give Back >50% of Daily Profit**
- Earned $400 by 10:30 AM → now down to $180 profit → EXIT ALL
- When volatility increases and you give back half = market conditions changed
- Protect your wins, don't get greedy

---

### ⚠️ **Soft Signals — Watch for Exit** (Technical)

**Candle Closes Below 9 EMA (1-min chart)**
- Price above EMA = uptrend intact
- Close below EMA = potential reversal
- Exit 25-50% and raise stop on remainder

**Volume Dries Up on UP Candles**
- Declining volume = momentum fading
- Example: First candles 500K volume, 5 candles later 100K volume
- Signal = buyers stepping away
- Exit on next small pullback

**MACD Histogram Turns Red (Positive → Negative)**
- Momentum indicator flips
- Not immediate sell, but warning
- Tighten stop, be ready to exit

**Stock Touches Prior Day's High**
- Tests resistance 2-3 times = accumulation phase over
- Example: Prior close $6.50, stock rallies to $7.30 (test), pulls back, tries again
- On 3rd test = less likely to break
- Exit 50% and trail stop on remainder

**Time Decay (11:00 AM+ approaching)**
- Probability of big moves declines after 10:30 AM
- More reversals, less follow-through
- If still in position at 10:45 AM and no major gains, exit or tighten stop

## Exit Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Kills Profits | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|------|
| Holding entire position to top | Greed — almost always reverses | Scale out at targets; let 25-50% trail |
| Moving stop lower (adding risk) | Turning winners into losers | NEVER move stop lower; only raise it |
| Holding through close for "overnight" | Day trader, not swing trader | Exit by 3:50 PM, no overnight holds |
| Ignoring stop loss (hoping) | Hope is not a strategy | Hit stop = exit immediately, period |
| Selling at small gain (panic) | Fear + good start = sells too early | Have targets pre-set; stick to plan |
| Revenge trading (trying to recover) | Desperation + poor decisions | Max daily loss rule = walk away |
| Watching too long after exit | Regret + FOMOing back in | Exit, step away 10 minutes, check next setup |
| Averaging down on losers | Adding to bad trades | BUY winners (scaling in on breakouts), not losers |
| No profit target set | Wandering, no edge | PRE-SET targets: 2:1, 3:1, resistance levels |
| Closing position 1 minute too early | Sold at $6.69, it went to $6.80 | Acceptable. Profit is profit. Move on. |

## Exit Decision Tree

```
IS MY STOP LOSS HIT?
├─ YES → EXIT ALL immediately (non-negotiable)
└─ NO → Go to next check

IS MY FIRST TARGET HIT (2:1 R/R)?
├─ YES → SELL 50%, move stop to breakeven on remainder
└─ NO → Go to next check

IS TECHNICAL SIGNAL SHOWING (EMA close, volume down, MACD flip)?
├─ YES → SELL 25-50%, raise stop on remainder
└─ NO → Go to next check

IS TIME DECAY APPROACHING (10:45+ AM)?
├─ YES → EXIT or reduce to 25% if major gains
└─ NO → Go to next check

HAVE I HIT MY DAILY MAX LOSS?
├─ YES → STOP TRADING, walk away (green-to-red rule)
└─ NO → Go to next check

AM I STILL UP 50%+ FROM FIRST PROFIT TARGET?
├─ YES → Keep trailing stop, let remainder run to 3:1 or resistance
└─ NO → Hold with trailing stop until signal, OR if no gains after 15 min from entry → exit

FINAL CHECK: Am I comfortable holding this?
├─ YES → Trail with 5-cent stop until signal
└─ NO → EXIT (trust your gut; fear is data)
```

## The Psychology of Selling

### ✅ **Healthy Selling Mindset**

1. **Profit = Success** — $100 profit is a WIN, not a miss
2. **Process Over Price** — Good execution on exit = good trade
3. **Sell into Strength** — Don't wait for reversal; take profits on way up
4. **Accept Regret** — Sold at $6.70, went to $6.85? That's OK. You made $150.
5. **Scaling Out Reduces Regret** — Keep 25-50% to catch big moves
6. **Time Discipline** — Most profit = first 2 hours; after 11 AM quality drops

### ❌ **Toxic Selling Mindset**

- "I could've made $300 if I held longer" = regret trap (leads to holding losers next time)
- "This stock is going to $10" = hope, not probability
- "I'll sell on the way back down" = most winners don't come back
- "Just 10 more cents" = greed trap (costs you $200 on next trade)

## Real Example: Correct Exit Execution

**Setup**: GAP-UP morning, 22% gain by 9:35 AM, all 5 pillars met
- Stock: $6.00 → $7.32 (22% gain)
- Entry point: $6.50 (caught pullback)
- Stop: $6.35 (15¢ below)
- Shares: 1,000
- Risk: $150 max loss

**Execution**:
- 9:40 AM: Price $6.70 (+$0.20 = 2:1 R/R) → SELL 500 shares = +$100 locked
- Move stop on remaining 500 to $6.50 (entry, breakeven)
- 9:45 AM: Price $6.90 (3:1 R/R at $6.80+) → SELL 250 shares = +$100
- Trail remaining 250 with 5¢ stop at $6.85
- 10:00 AM: Price touches $7.05 (high), volume starts to decline
- Candle closes at $6.95, below 9 EMA signal → EXIT final 250 = +$112
- **Total Profit: $312 on $6,500 investment in 20 minutes**
- **Result: 4.8% gain, excellent execution**

**Key Points**:
- Didn't wait for $1.00 move (greed)
- Took profits at logical levels
- Protected gains with scaling
- Exited on technical signal, not desperation
- Total time: 20 minutes (best window)
- 3 separate exits = reduced regret

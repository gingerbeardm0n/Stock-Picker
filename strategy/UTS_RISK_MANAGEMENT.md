## Core Principle: Risk, Not Reward

**You control risk. You don't control reward.**

- You CANNOT predict how much a stock will move
- You CAN predict exactly how much you will lose (via stop loss)
- Therefore, build every trade around your MAXIMUM LOSS, not your target gain

## Position Sizing Formula

### The Golden Rule

```
Position Size = Max Loss Per Trade / Stop Loss Distance (in dollars)

Example:
  Max Loss = $200
  Stop Loss = 10 cents ($0.10) below entry
  Shares = $200 / $0.10 = 2,000 shares
```

### Key Calculations

**1. Max Loss Per Trade (Recommended: 2-3% of Account)**

| Account Size | 2% Risk | 3% Risk | Typical Daily Max |
|-------------|---------|---------|------------------|
| $3,000 | $60 | $90 | $200-$300 |
| $5,000 | $100 | $150 | $300-$500 |
| $10,000 | $200 | $300 | $500-$1,000 |
| $25,000+ | $500 | $750 | $1,000-$2,500 |

**2. Stop Loss Distance (Typical: 10-15 cents)**

Most momentum setups have natural stops:
- Bull flag: 1-2 cents below flag low
- ABCD pattern: Below point B low
- Pullback: 1-2 cents below dip low
- Typical: 5-15 cents (10¢ is most common)

**3. Calculate Shares**

```
IF max_loss = $200 AND stop = 10¢:
  Shares = $200 / $0.10 = 2,000 shares

IF max_loss = $100 AND stop = 5¢:
  Shares = $100 / $0.05 = 2,000 shares

IF max_loss = $300 AND stop = 15¢:
  Shares = $300 / $0.15 = 2,000 shares
```

## Daily Rules (The 5 Rules for Selling Losers)

### ✅ **Rule 1: Daily Max Loss = Daily Profit Target**

**Once you've lost X amount, you're done for the day.**

Example:
- Your goal: Make $300 profit today
- Daily Max Loss: $300
- If you hit -$300 loss BEFORE hitting +$300 profit → STOP TRADING
- If you hit +$300 profit BEFORE hitting -$300 loss → STOP TRADING

**Why**: Protects your account during inevitable losing streaks. Some days, the market isn't set up well. Accept it and preserve capital.

---

### ✅ **Rule 2: Green-to-Red Rule** (The Emotional Breaker)

**If your account turns RED (goes from profitable to losing), STOP TRADING immediately.**

Timeline:
- 9:30-10:15 AM: Made +$200 profit (up)
- 10:20 AM: Lost $150 on a bad trade (now at +$50)
- 10:30 AM: Lost another $100 (now at -$50, RED)
- **ACTION: STOP TRADING. Walk away for the day.**

**Why**: Once you lose money after being green, psychology breaks. Revenge trading cascades losses. Your edge returns when you're calm tomorrow.

---

### ✅ **Rule 3: Give-Back-Half Rule** (The Greed Breaker)

**If you hit your daily goal, then lose more than HALF of it, EXIT all remaining positions immediately.**

Example:
- Daily profit target: $400
- Hit $400 by 10:45 AM
- Market volatility picks up, you lose $200 (down to $200 profit)
- **ACTION: Close everything. Protect the $200. Walk away.**

**Why**: When volatility increases enough to give back 50% of profits, market conditions have changed. The easy money is gone. Don't force it.

---

### ✅ **Rule 4: Quality Stocks Only** (The Discipline Rule)

**Only trade stocks that meet ALL 5 Pillars.**

- Never trade a "B-quality" setup
- If float is 45M, skip it (even if rest of setup looks good)
- If volume is only 2x, skip it
- If you can't identify a catalyst, skip it

A-quality only:
- All 5 pillars confirmed
- Float <20M (ideal <5-10M)
- Relative volume 5x+ (10x+ preferred)
- Clear support/resistance
- News catalyst confirmed

**Why**: 2-3 A-quality trades beat 10-15 mediocre ones every time. Your win rate is higher on A+ setups.

---

### ✅ **Rule 5: Trade Less** (The Volume Rule)

**Take 2-4 high-conviction trades per day, not 10-15 impulsive ones.**

- Ross's data: 100% of net profit came from $0-$4,000 trades (base hits)
- Most profitable traders: 3-5 trades/day at high quality
- Most losing traders: 15-20 trades/day at low quality

The math:
```
A-Quality Trader:
  3 trades/day
  60% win rate
  $150 avg win, $100 avg loss
  Expected daily = (1.8 wins * $150) - (1.2 losses * $100) = $270 - $120 = +$150/day

B-Quality Trader:
  15 trades/day
  50% win rate
  $80 avg win, $100 avg loss
  Expected daily = (7.5 wins * $80) - (7.5 losses * $100) = $600 - $750 = -$150/day
```

**Why**: Quality over quantity. Fewer trades = fewer mistakes, better focus, less emotional exhaustion.

## Small Account Strategy ($3,000-$5,000)

### ✅ **Position Cap: 5,000 Shares Until First $1,000 Profit**

Once you've made your first $1,000 cumulative profit, you can increase position size.

**Why**:
- $3,000 account: First trades use 5,000 share max to stay in small risk range
- Example: Entry $6.50, stop $6.40 (10¢) → 5,000 shares max
  - Max loss = $500 (16.7% of account) — **too much**
  - Better: 2,000 shares max = $200 loss (6.7% of account) — **better**
- Once you reach $4,000 (first $1,000 profit), you can use larger positions

**Calculation for Small Accounts**:
```
Max Loss = $100 (safer for $3-5K accounts)
Stop = 10¢
Shares = $100 / $0.10 = 1,000 shares

OR:
Max Loss = $150
Stop = 10¢
Shares = 1,500 shares
```

## Leverage & Margin (DO NOT USE)

### ⛔ **Rule: No Margin, No Leverage**

- Ross does NOT use leverage
- Day traders with leverage blow accounts
- Every 50% account swing is terrifying on leverage
- Stick to cash: 1,000 shares @ $6.50 = $6,500 (need $6,500 cash)

**Why**:
- Leverage amplifies losses
- Broker can liquidate on bad fills
- Psychological pressure increases 2-3x
- Not needed for day trading

## Money Management Psychology

### ✅ **Healthy Mindset**

1. **Small Wins Compound** — $200/day * 250 trading days = $50,000/year
2. **Consistency Beats Hitting Home Runs** — $300/day is better than $3,000 once and -$2,500 another day
3. **Capital Preservation** — Losing $500 = need to make $600 to get back to breakeven (10% haircut)
4. **Rules Protect You** — Daily max loss, position size, quality-only filter them protect against bad days
5. **Patience Pays** — Good setups come 5+ times per day; you can skip bad ones and wait

### ❌ **Toxic Mindset**

- "I just need one home run" — Leads to oversizing and blowing up
- "I'll make it back tomorrow" — FOMO trading after losses
- "Let's see what happens" — No stop loss = no edge
- "I'm so good, I can trade 10M float stocks" — Hubris before big loss
- "Rules are for beginners" — Professionals follow rules more strictly

## Common Account Killer Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| No position size rule | Oversizing leads to blown accounts | Position size = max_loss / stop distance |
| Ignoring daily max loss | One bad day destroys months of gains | Stop trading when limit hit |
| Adding to losers | Turns $200 loss into $500 loss | Add to winners only (scaling in on breakouts) |
| No R/R minimum | Bad odds = losing long-term | Require 2:1 minimum on every trade |
| Using margin/leverage | 50% account swing becomes 100% swing | Never use leverage on day trades |
| Trading after 11 AM | Low probability setups = losses | Focus trading 9:30-11 AM |
| Overtrading low-float/low-vol stocks | Can't exit when you need to | Min 500K daily avg volume |
| Not taking losses | Turns small loss into big loss | Hit stop loss immediately, no exceptions |
| Trading every setup | Exhaustion + mistakes increase | Limit to 2-4 high-quality trades |
| Ignoring the 5 pillars | Statistically weaker trades | Trade only A+ setups |

## Position Sizing Examples (Real Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: Small Account ($3,000), Conservative

```
Account: $3,000
Daily Profit Target: $200
Daily Max Loss: $200 (acceptable)
Risk Per Trade: $100 (safer)

Trade Opportunity:
  Stock: XYZ
  Entry: $6.50
  Stop: $6.35 (15¢ below)

Calculation:
  Max Loss = $100
  Stop Distance = $0.15
  Shares = $100 / $0.15 = 666 shares

Validation:
  Cost: 666 × $6.50 = $4,329 (exceeds account!)
  Solution: Use max position size of $3,000 (accounts for margin buffer)
  Shares = $3,000 / $6.50 = 461 shares
  If stopped: Loss = 461 × $0.15 = $69.15 ✓ (under $100 max)
```

### Scenario 2: Medium Account ($10,000), Moderate

```
Account: $10,000
Daily Profit Target: $300-$500
Daily Max Loss: $500 (acceptable, 5% of account)
Risk Per Trade: $150-$250 (2-2.5%)

Trade Opportunity:
  Stock: ABC (strong float <10M, 15x volume)
  Entry: $8.00
  Stop: $7.90 (10¢ below)

Calculation:
  Max Loss = $200
  Stop Distance = $0.10
  Shares = $200 / $0.10 = 2,000 shares

Validation:
  Cost: 2,000 × $8.00 = $16,000 (exceeds $10K account)
  Solution: Reduce max loss or find tighter stop
  Try Max Loss = $100: Shares = 1,000
  Cost: 1,000 × $8.00 = $8,000 ✓ (within account)
```

### Scenario 3: Large Account ($50,000), Aggressive

```
Account: $50,000
Daily Profit Target: $1,000-$1,500
Daily Max Loss: $1,500 (3% of account)
Risk Per Trade: $500-$750 (1-1.5% per trade)

Trade Opportunity:
  Stock: DEF (float <5M, 20x volume, earnings catalyst)
  Entry: $12.00
  Stop: $11.85 (15¢ below, tight support)

Calculation:
  Max Loss = $600
  Stop Distance = $0.15
  Shares = $600 / $0.15 = 4,000 shares

Validation:
  Cost: 4,000 × $12.00 = $48,000 ✓ (within account)
  Target 1: $12.30 (30¢ = 2:1 R/R) → Sell 2,000 shares = +$600
  Target 2: $12.60 (60¢ = 4:1 R/R) → Sell 1,000 shares = +$600
  Trailing: Final 1,000 shares with trailing stop
```

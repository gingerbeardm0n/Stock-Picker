## Pre-Entry Checklist (Before You Press Buy)

### ✅ ALL 5 Pillars Must Be Met Simultaneously
1. Price $2-$20 (sweet spot $5-$15)
2. Up 10%+ from prior close (by market open or premarket)
3. Relative Volume 5x+ (10x+ preferred, measured at time of entry)
4. Float <20M shares (ideally <5M-10M)
5. News catalyst (understand WHY the stock moved)

**If ANY pillar is missing, SKIP the trade.** There will be another stock.

### ✅ Technical Confirmation
- Stock must be trending UP (not sideways or down)
- Volume on UP candles > volume on DOWN candles
- Price above 9 EMA (1-min chart)
- MACD shows positive momentum (histogram > 0)

### ✅ Risk Setup
- Identify clear stop loss location (exact price in cents)
- Calculate max loss ($60-$300 depending on account size)
- Calculate shares = max_loss / stop_loss_distance
- Risk/Reward ratio ≥ 2:1 (if stop is 10¢, target must be ≥20¢)

**If you can't identify a clean stop or R/R is <2:1, SKIP.**

## Entry Patterns (Ranked by Reliability)

### 1. **Bull Flag** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Highest Probability)

**Setup**:
- Stock gaps up and rips higher with strong volume (flagpole)
- Flagpole = first 1-3 green candles, high volume, strong closing
- Then consolidates: 2-3 red candles on LIGHT volume (pullback)
- Pullback must NOT break the flagpole low (support)

**Entry**:
- Enter on the FIRST green candle that breaks the flag resistance
- Volume on breakout must match or exceed flagpole volume
- Stop loss = 1-2 cents below the flag support (flagpole low)
- Target = prior high + (flagpole height) = often 50-100 cents

**Why it works**:
- Light volume pullback = weak sellers, buyers still in control
- Breakout = resumption of institutional buying
- Most reliable pattern Ross trades

**Example**: Stock gaps up $2.00 at open (flagpole). Pulls back 30¢ on light volume (2-3 red candles). First green candle from flag bottom, above flag resistance = BUY. Stop 2¢ below flag low. Target 50¢+ above entry.

---

### 2. **Micro Pullback / First Candle to New High** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Reliable)

**Setup**:
- Stock is in strong uptrend (green candles, higher highs)
- Pauses for 1-2 candles (slight pullback, light volume)
- Next candle = first green close above the high of the pause

**Entry**:
- Enter on the candle that makes a new high above the previous high
- Stop loss = just below the pullback low (1-2 cents)
- Target = 20-50 cents above entry (or previous high + similar leg)

**Why it works**:
- Pullback attracts new buyers at lower price
- New high = continuation of trend
- Tight stop = low risk

**Example**: Stock consolidating at $6.50-$6.60 on light volume. Next green candle breaks $6.60 high = BUY. Stop at $6.48. Target $6.85-$7.00.

---

### 3. **ABCD Pattern** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Textbook Momentum)

**Setup**:
- A-to-B: Strong rally (flagpole equivalent, 30¢-$1+ move)
- B: Pullback (point B = the dip, may be 20-40% of A-to-B move)
- B-to-C: Rally from B back toward A (creates ascending support line)
- C: Secondary pullback (point C = another dip, on light volume)
- **Critical**: Point D must NOT break below point B low (support)

**Entry**:
- Enter on candle that breaks the C resistance (trending back up from D)
- Stop loss = just below point B (or point D if D is barely above B)
- Target = point A high (or above, if momentum continues)

**Why it works**:
- Tests support twice (B and D) without breaking = strong buyers
- Two ascending lows (B > D impossible, but D not breaking B) = floor
- Pattern predicts continuation to prior highs

**Example**:
- A-to-B: $5.00 → $6.50 (flagpole, +$1.50)
- B pullback: $6.50 → $6.00 (retest of lower support)
- B-to-C: $6.00 → $6.40 (rally back up)
- C pullback: $6.40 → $6.10 (secondary dip, light volume)
- D breakout: $6.10 → break $6.40 high = BUY
- Stop: $5.95 (below B low)
- Target: $6.50+ (A high or beyond)

---

### 4. **Dip Buy (The 3 Tricks)** ⭐⭐⭐ (Intermediate, Requires Skill)

**Setup**:
- Stock in strong uptrend, pauses on light volume
- Dip = small pullback, but with 3 confirming indicators:
  1. Light volume on pullback candles
  2. Price stays above 9 EMA (1-min chart)
  3. MACD histogram remains positive (green bars, or crossing zero upward)

**Entry**:
- Buy the dip when all 3 tricks confirmed
- Stop loss = 1-2 cents below the dip low
- Target = prior high + extension

**Why it works**:
- 3 tricks = multiple confirmation of underlying strength
- Light volume = weak selling pressure
- MACD+ = momentum still intact
- EMA above = price structure intact

**Example**: Stock at $7.50, pulls back to $7.35 (15¢ dip). Check: volume light ✓, above 9 EMA ✓, MACD green ✓ = BUY at $7.35+. Stop $7.33. Target $7.60-$7.75.

**Risk**: Requires more skill to identify the 3 tricks. Don't use until comfortable.

---

### 5. **Flat Top Breakout** ⭐⭐⭐ (Consolidation Play)

**Setup**:
- Stock consolidates at resistance, touching the same price 2-3 times
- Each touch = lower volume than previous touch (or buyers stepping in)
- Flat top = horizontal resistance line (all touches within 2-3 cents)

**Entry**:
- Enter on the volume spike that breaks above the flat top
- Volume must be higher than previous consolidation volume
- Stop loss = just below the flat top low

**Why it works**:
- Multiple tests of resistance = building pressure
- Each touch without breaking = accumulation by smart buyers
- Volume breakout = institutions entering

**Example**: Stock consolidates at $8.45-$8.50 for 5 minutes, three touches on light volume. Sudden volume spike, candle breaks $8.50 = BUY. Stop $8.42. Target $8.75-$9.00.

## Time-of-Day Entry Rules

### ✅ **Best Entry Window: 9:30-10:30 AM EST** (Peak hunting hour)
- Highest probability of sustained moves
- Most volume = best liquidity
- News catalysts confirmed by this time

### ⚠️ **Good Window: 10:30 AM-12:00 PM EST** (Still tradeable)
- Trend established, fewer surprises
- Lower volume = tighter spreads, but slower fills
- Still take high-conviction setups

### 🛑 **Avoid: 12:00-4:00 PM EST** (Midday lull)
- Low volume = wide spreads, choppy price action
- Institutions away at lunch
- "Dead zone" — most losses in afternoon

### 📈 **Premarket Watch: 7:00-9:30 AM EST**
- Don't trade, just SCOUT
- Identify candidates that gapped up
- Plan entries for open bell
- Note: Premarket liquidity is thin — don't go live in PM

## Entry Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Kills Trades | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Buying at the TOP (50%+ move already) | FOMO — reversals happen | Wait for pullback + breakout pattern |
| Ignoring float >20M | Slow/choppy moves, hard to exit | SKIP — wait for another stock |
| Entering on red candle | Buying weakness, not strength | Wait for first green after setup |
| No clear stop loss | Holding losers, account bleed | ALWAYS identify stop before buying |
| Chasing with bad R/R (<2:1) | Odds stacked against you | Pass, another setup will be better |
| Trading bad float/low volume | Impossible to exit | Filter ruthlessly — only A+ setups |
| Overtrading low-cap stocks (<$1M avg vol) | Slippage kills profits | Minimum 500K daily avg volume |
| Entering on declining volume | Momentum fading | Volume must be UP or maintained |
| Ignoring the 5 pillars (just one missing) | Statistically inferior setups | ALL 5 or SKIP |

## Quick Pattern Recognition

**See flagpole (1-3 green candles, high volume)?**
→ Wait for light volume pullback → Enter on first green above flag = **Bull Flag**

**See stock trending up, pause 1-2 candles, then green to new high?**
→ Enter on new high = **Micro Pullback**

**See pull back from resistance, rally back, dip again, first green from dip?**
→ Check if dip holds below prior rally high = **ABCD Pattern**

**See three touches of same price, lower volume each touch, then volume spike breakout?**
→ Enter on volume breakout = **Flat Top**

**See downtrend slowing, volume light, price above EMA, MACD green?**
→ Enter the pullback = **Dip Buy**

# Concept: Gap and Go

**Last updated:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** Full corpus analysis — TRANSCRIPT_SUMMARIES_0001-1799 (all 1,799 sessions)  
**Sample size:** 1,177+ gap-and-go trades (23% of all parsed trades — most frequent pattern)  
**Win rate:** 69% (812W / 338L of 1,165 trades with outcome data)

---

## Definition

Gap-and-go is Ross's highest-frequency setup. A stock gaps up significantly premarket, holds above VWAP or premarket support, and continues the momentum move at or near the open. The entry is the break of the premarket high — not a pullback, not a dip buy — the continuation of upward momentum.

**Key distinction from other patterns:**
- **vs. micro-pullback:** gap-and-go enters on the BREAK; micro-pullback enters on the DIP after the initial move
- **vs. halt-resume:** gap-and-go is open-driven (or catalyst-driven premarket); halt-resume occurs mid-session after a circuit breaker halt
- **vs. red-to-green:** gap-and-go stays green all day; red-to-green recovers from negative territory

## Setup Criteria

### Required
| Criterion | Target | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Gap% | 10%+ minimum; 20%+ preferred; 40-300%+ ideal | 7-15% gaps exist in data but lower conviction; high gap% = faster move |
| Premarket volume | High/very-high relative volume | Low rel-vol gapper = weak setup; skip or reduce size |
| Price holding above VWAP or prior-day high | Clear hold = strong | Weak hold = skip |

### Strongly Preferred (not hard requirements)
| Criterion | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| News catalyst | AI announcement, biotech data, earnings beat, sympathy play, merger — adds urgency and traps shorts. Not required — pure volume/momentum gaps work, but lower conviction |
| Low float (sub-1M to 4M) | Low float = bigger moves per share; high-float names move slower and are harder to scalp |
| Clean premarket high level | Whole dollar, half dollar = obvious target that the market watches |

**News vs. no-news:** Many large gap-and-go winners were "no news" trades (SBFM +$20K, OCEA +$11K, WETG +$18K). Ross trades pure momentum gaps when the vol/float/setup is compelling. The difference: news setups allow more size and longer holds; no-news gaps get smaller size and faster exits.

## Entry Trigger Variants

### Variant 1: Break of Premarket High (Primary, Most Common)
1. Identify the premarket high on the chart
2. Wait for price to consolidate near that level (micro-pullback or flat base)
3. Enter on the CANDLE that closes above premarket high
4. Confirm with volume — the break should come on increased tape activity

### Variant 2: First 5-Minute Candle to New High
- Used when stock gaps up but is too volatile at open
- Wait for the first 5-minute candle to close, then enter the break of that candle's high
- Provides more confirmation, slightly later entry, better defined stop
- Seen in trades: WORX (no news, +$2,900), SNOA (+$24,000)

### Variant 3: "4-Second Open" Aggressive Entry
- Ultra-aggressive variant: stock gaps up strongly, immediately rips at open
- Ross enters within the first few seconds of 9:30 if it's already breaking premarket high
- Requires very high premarket volume and obvious momentum
- Seen in: BPTH 43% gap — "first 1-min candle new high within 4 seconds"
- Higher risk due to opening volatility spike; reserved for strongest setups

### Variant 4: Premarket Entry (Before 9:30)
- When catalyst is strong and stock has established premarket momentum
- Valid if premarket volume is exceptional and direction is clear
- Risk: gaps can reverse at open; use smaller size, tight stop at premarket support
- Time range: 8:00am–9:29am
- Seen in: AUD (AI news at 7am), HTGM after-hours squeeze

### Variant 5: Multi-Day Continuation
- Stock gapped on Day 1, still has momentum on Day 2-3
- Entry same mechanics — break of prior session high or VWAP
- Watch for declining interest: fewer halts, lower relative volume = warning sign
- Seen in: SST continuation from prior day (+$13,500 on halt-resume)

## Add-On Mechanics

Based on 1,177 trades, most common add-on approaches:

| Mechanic | Frequency | Description |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Scaling into strength | Most common | Add shares as price proves the move at breakout levels |
| Adds on dips to 9-MA | Common | Dip to the 9-period moving average = add opportunity during extended moves |
| $3K → $6K → $9K scaling | Common | Ross builds position in tranches as conviction grows |
| Sympathy play add | Moderate | Secondary stock in same sector is trading — add to leading name on its dip |
| Quick scalp (no adds) | ~15% | Pure scalp with no adds — smaller size, faster exit |

**Key principle:** Ross sizes INTO strength, not against it. If the move stalls, he stops adding. If it dips to 9-MA and holds, that's an add opportunity — not a reason to exit.

**Front-side vs. back-side:** Ross is aggressive on the FRONT SIDE of a move (first leg, stock making new highs, fresh breakout). He becomes cautious on the BACK SIDE (stock has already run far, momentum slowing, late entry). Early-leg momentum is where size belongs.

## Stop Criteria

| Stop Type | Frequency | When to Apply |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Failed breakout — close back below premarket high | Most common | Price breaks level then immediately falls back below it — hard stop |
| Reversal / selling pressure | Common | Tape flips from buying to selling — scale out |
| Below VWAP | Moderate | VWAP reclaim fails — exit partial or full |
| Halt-down | Occasional | Circuit breaker halt to downside — exit all on resume |
| False breakout (multiple attempts, no follow) | Common | Level gets tested repeatedly but can't hold — exit |

**Stop placement:** Below the premarket high level that was broken (the trigger level becomes support). If price reclaims, Ross will often re-enter.

**Critical rule from summaries:** Ross holds through SMALL flushes if the overall structure is intact, but exits immediately on failed breakouts that close back below the breakout level. The wick of FILE 1017 (MSGM: unrealized -$17K) shows the risk of holding too long in extended premarket entries.

## Profit Targets

| Target Type | Notes |
|-------------|-------|
| Whole-dollar levels | $2.00, $3.00, $4.00, $5.00 — key psychological resistance |
| Half-dollar levels | $4.50, $7.50, $8.50 — secondary targets |
| Resistance from chart | Prior highs, pre-break consolidation zones |
| Scaling exits | Ross rarely takes a single exit — scales out 25-50% at each target |

**Observed T1 values:** $4.20, $7.50, $8.00+, $6.50, $3.50, $5.00+, $14.00, $11.00, $4.90 — spread suggests these are stock-specific, not fixed levels.

**Important:** For stocks with multi-dollar breakout potential, do NOT use the premarket high as the profit target. It is the ENTRY reference, not the exit. The profit target comes from the next significant resistance zone.

## Sympathy Plays

When a leading gapper is running hard, nearby stocks in the same sector often move in sympathy. Ross regularly trades the leading name but notes sympathy setups on secondary movers.

**Examples:**
- GBR moved on APOP's gap-and-go strength (same session)
- ZIS +$3,331 on HTGM's morning run
- ALT +$2,600 on BPTH's gap-and-go

**Rule:** Trade the sympathy play the same way as the primary gap-and-go (break of its own high, volume confirmation) — not blindly. Sympathy plays that fail to confirm are a LOSS (FILE 0200-0299: COHN sympathy attempt → -$900 "no sympathy").

## Hold Duration

| Duration | % of gap-and-go trades |
|----------|------------------------|
| Short (5-30 min) | 48% |
| Scalp (<5 min) | 38% |
| Extended (30min+) | 10% |
| Unknown | 4% |

**Interpretation:** Gap-and-go is primarily a short-term setup. Ross takes quick profits when momentum is obvious, extending hold time only when the stock has exceptional relative strength. Extended holds (10%) correspond to the $20K-$50K+ days — but require continuous confirmation (halts up, new highs, strong volume). 

The 10% extended stat likely understates truly exceptional setups where the stock goes multi-day (e.g., $5→$21 in a single session for prediction-markets news play, scaling throughout).

## MACD State

MACD state is **rarely documented** for gap-and-go entries and is **not used as a filter**:
- Unknown: 66%
- Not populated (-): 30%
- Positive: 4%
- Negative: <1% (and still wins when news/momentum strong enough)

**Interpretation:** Ross does not use MACD as a primary filter for gap-and-go. The setup is momentum/news-driven. MACD being negative does NOT disqualify a gap-and-go. This contrasts with the micro-pullback pattern where MACD state matters more.

## Market Temperature Dependency

Gap-and-go performance is strongly correlated with market temperature:

- **Hot days:** Ross enters larger size, adds more aggressively, holds longer. Multiple gap-and-go entries possible. "Fourth week of strong momentum" → $5,200+ day with multiple winners.
- **Neutral days:** Normal size, normal conviction. Take setups that meet all criteria.
- **Cold days:** Same stocks, same setups, but stops hit more frequently. Size-reduce. Only take the A+ catalyst with highest premarket volume. Exit at T1 (full position).
- **Chop days:** One-and-done. If first gap-and-go fails, done for the session.

---

## Red Flags (When to Skip)

- Gap is small (<10%) on a non-news, non-volatile stock
- Premarket volume is **low** relative to float — low-vol gaps fail without buyers
- Stock already ran 2x+ premarket and has no fresh catalyst at open (back-side entry risk)
- Level 2 shows heavy sellers stacked at premarket high (resistance won't break cleanly)
- Broad market is down (indices red) — gap-and-go setups fail more frequently
- Stock is a large-float name (>10M shares) — moves are slower, harder to scalp
- After 10:30am — morning momentum has decayed (later cutoff on hot days)
- **Reverse split gaps:** stock gapped because of a reverse split, not actual buying interest — these fail more frequently (STOP: reversal - full flush observed in data)
- **Chased entry:** stock already ran far from its premarket high when you enter — "chased gap-and-go / impulse entry" is an explicit failure mode (FILE 655: AKTX -$2,800; FILE 0600-0699: NOVVN -$1,800 same reason)

## jTrader Decision Rules

```
GAP_AND_GO entry conditions:
  - gap% >= 10% (prefer >= 20%, ideal >= 40%)
  - premarket relative volume = HIGH or VERY_HIGH (low = skip)
  - price approaching or at premarket high
  - time < 10:30am ET (cold day); < 11:00am ET (hot/neutral day)
  
  news catalyst = preferred but not required:
    IF news: allow larger position (full max_position_pct)
    IF no news: reduce size by 25-33%
  
  IF float < 5M: allow full position size
  ELSE IF float < 20M: allow 75% of max position size
  ELSE (large float): reduce size by 50%

Entry trigger (primary — break of premarket high):
  - 1m candle CLOSES above premarket high
  - volume on that candle > 1.5x average candle volume
  
Entry trigger (variant — first 5-minute candle):
  - wait for 9:35 AM
  - 5m candle closes above its own high
  - lower size than primary trigger (confirmation-based)

Stop:
  - initial stop = below premarket high (the broken level)
  - if price closes back below trigger level = full exit

Targets:
  - T1 = next whole or half dollar above entry
  - scale 25-50% at T1, hold rest
  - T2 = next significant resistance or next whole/half dollar
  - extend hold only if stock is halting up on increasing volume

Red flags to skip:
  - entry price already far above premarket high (chased)
  - low relative volume
  - reverse split catalyst (not real buying)
  - after 10:30am (cold day) / 11:00am (neutral)
  - broad market red (indices down)

Market temperature gate:
  - hot market: full size, hold to T2+
  - neutral market: normal size, T1 then reassess  
  - cold market: 50% size, exit full position at T1
  - chop: skip borderline setups, smallest size on A+ only
```

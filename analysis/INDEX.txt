DATA FILES
----------

  daily_signals.csv (279 rows, 14 columns)
    Key columns:
      - date: trading day
      - regime: actual regime (BREAKOUT, PULLBACK, REVERSAL, UNKNOWN)
      - num_trades: total trades that day
      - num_unique_symbols: count of distinct symbols
      - avg_entry_price: average entry price
      - avg_price_move_pct: average % move (exit - entry) / entry
      - avg_hold_minutes: average hold time
      - win_rate_pct: % of winning trades
      - total_pnl: sum of daily P&L
      - avg_pnl_per_trade: average P&L per trade
      - dominant_pattern: most common pattern

    Use for: Visualizations, trend analysis, custom analysis

  daily_signals_with_detection.csv (279 rows, 18 columns)
    Extends daily_signals.csv with:
      - num_trades_ma30: 30-day moving average
      - num_unique_symbols_ma30: 30-day moving average
      - regime_detected: detected regime using simple rules
      - (plus rolling averages at windows 1,3,5,15,30 for all signals)

    Use for: Evaluating detection accuracy

  correlation_summary.csv (40 rows, 5 columns)
    Shows Pearson correlation of each signal vs regime type
    Columns: window, signal, pearson_r, abs_pearson, strength, direction

    Strongest signals:
      1. num_unique_symbols at 30-day: r=0.5090 (STRONG)
      2. num_trades at 30-day: r=0.3529 (MODERATE)

    Use for: Understanding which signals predict regime

  regime_profiles.csv (4 rows)
    Summary statistics for each regime (BREAKOUT, PULLBACK, REVERSAL, UNKNOWN)
    Shows average: trades/day, symbols, price move, win rate, daily P&L

    Use for: Quick reference of regime characteristics

OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This analysis examined 278 trading days across 13 months to identify which
internal market condition signals best predict which of 3 trading regimes we
are currently in:

  BREAKOUT (166 days):   Jan, Feb, Jun, Aug, Sep, Oct, Dec '25 + Jan '26
  PULLBACK (85 days):    Mar, Apr, May, Jul '25
  REVERSAL (19 days):    Nov '25

The goal was to find signals that can automatically detect regime changes
so we can switch between optimized trading configs in real-time.


================================================================================
                          CRITICAL FINDINGS
================================================================================

FINDING #1: MARKET BREADTH IS THE STRONGEST REGIME INDICATOR
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Signal: Number of Unique Symbols (market breadth)
Correlation at 30-day lookback: r = 0.5090 (STRONGEST)

What this means:
  - BREAKOUT regimes have diverse candidates (avg 11.93 symbols/day)
  - PULLBACK regimes have fewer setups (avg 8.86 symbols/day)
  - Difference: 3+ more candidate stocks per day in BREAKOUT

Why it works:
  When multiple unrelated stocks are screening simultaneously, it indicates
  broad market strength and breakout conditions. During consolidation/pullback
  periods, fewer stocks meet the 5-pillar criteria.

Implementation:
  Compute 30-day rolling average of daily unique symbols
  Threshold: > 10 = BREAKOUT likely
             < 9 = PULLBACK/REVERSAL likely


FINDING #2: TRADING VOLUME / ACTIVITY CONFIRMS REGIME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Signal: Total Number of Trades per Day
Correlation at 30-day lookback: r = 0.3529 (SECOND STRONGEST)

What this means:
  - BREAKOUT regimes: ~100 trades/day (high activity)
  - PULLBACK regimes: ~64 trades/day (lower activity)
  - Difference: 50% more trading opportunities in BREAKOUT

Why it works:
  More candidates = more patterns triggered = more trades
  This is a consequence of breadth, not a primary signal

Implementation:
  Use as secondary confirmation
  Threshold: > 80 trades/day = BREAKOUT
             < 70 trades/day = PULLBACK


FINDING #3: 30-DAY LOOKBACK IS OPTIMAL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lookback analysis:
  1-day:   r = 0.20 (too noisy, daily variance)
  3-day:   r = 0.22 (still noisy)
  5-day:   r = 0.23 (moderate)
  15-day:  r = 0.30 (good)
  30-day:  r = 0.51 (BEST)

Interpretation:
  Regime changes unfold over ~1 month, not days
  Daily regime signals would trigger false switches
  Use monthly-level decisions based on rolling 30-day averages


FINDING #4: WHAT DOESN'T WORK (SURPRISINGLY)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

These signals have NO predictive power for regime (all r < 0.01):
  - Win rate percentage (r = -0.004)
  - Daily total P&L (r = -0.020)
  - Average entry price level (r = 0.006)

These have weak predictive power (r < 0.15):
  - Average price movement per trade (r = 0.018)
  - Average hold time (r = 0.130)
  - Average P&L per trade (r = 0.068)

Why this matters:
  Regime detection is about BREADTH/OPPORTUNITY, not profitability
  You can't detect regime by looking at your P&L - it's self-referential
  Must use opportunity signals (candidate count, activity) instead


FINDING #5: CURRENT SIMPLE DETECTION METHOD: 43% ACCURACY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Using the simple rule:
  IF (symbols_30d > 10) AND (trades_30d > 80) THEN BREAKOUT
  IF (symbols_30d < 9) AND (trades_30d < 70) THEN PULLBACK
  ELSE TRANSITION

Results:
  Overall accuracy: 43.2% (120/278 days correct)

  By regime:
    BREAKOUT detection: 37% (62/166 detected correctly)
    PULLBACK detection: 68% (58/85 detected correctly) - GOOD
    REVERSAL detection: 0% (0/19 detected correctly) - Too small sample

Problem analysis:
  - Early month has high variance in rolling averages
  - Many BREAKOUT days get classified as TRANSITION (not bad - neutral)
  - PULLBACK detection works well
  - Need more sophisticated model (e.g., logistic regression, decision tree)

================================================================================
                    KEY INSIGHTS FOR TRADER
================================================================================

1. BREADTH MATTERS
   When many unrelated stocks screen, market is strong (BREAKOUT).
   When few candidates pass filters, market is weak (PULLBACK).
   This is your #1 regime indicator - watch it weekly.

2. YOUR BIGGEST GAINS COME FROM REGIME SELECTION
   Theoretical ceiling: 607% improvement from regime switching
   Even with imperfect detection, expected gain: +$3,000-3,900
   This is BIGGER than any individual pattern optimization.

3. PULLBACK MONTHS ARE PREDICTABLE
   Your algorithm detected PULLBACK with 68% accuracy already.
   This is the month type you LOSE money in (avg -$1,852).
   Even basic detection helps you de-risk those months.

4. REVERSAL MONTH IS RARE AND SMALL
   Only Nov (1 month, 19 days). Don't over-optimize for it.
   Focus on BREAKOUT/PULLBACK split which covers 85% of year.

5. EARLY-MONTH CHAOS IS NORMAL
   In early January, symbol counts vary wildly (rolling average ramp-up).
   This causes false signals in first 30 days of analysis.
   Consider: Only apply regime rules starting month 2.

6. MONITOR THE META-SIGNAL
   Check your symbols_ma30 every Monday.
   If it's stuck between 9-10, market is in transition - be cautious.
   Sharp breaks above 10 or below 9 signal regime shift.

# Ross Cameron Momentum Day Trading - Strategic Overview

**Source**: ULTIMATE TRADING STRATEGY.pdf (synthesized from 75+ video transcripts)

## Core Philosophy

**Trading is a BUSINESS, not gambling.** Success = consistent process execution + disciplined risk management + patience for best setups.

**Base Hits Over Home Runs**: Data shows $0-$4,000 profit trades = 100% of net profit ($354K over 10 months). Home runs and big losers cancel each other. Focus on many small wins ($200-$400 each), not chasing moonshots.

## The 5 Pillars (ALL Must Be Met Simultaneously)

**Pillar 1: Price Range ($2-$20)**
- Sweet spot: $5-$15
- Under $2: wide spreads, low liquidity
- Over $20: small percentage moves, more capital needed
- See: UTS_ENTRY_RULES.md Section 1

**Pillar 2: Up 10%+ on the Day**
- Minimum 10%, ideal 15-30% from prior close
- Proves institutional buying interest
- Creates conditions for continuation moves
- See: UTS_ENTRY_RULES.md Section 2

**Pillar 3: Relative Volume (5x-10x+ Average)**
- Minimum 5x (14-day average), strong setups 10x+
- Best indicator of sustained move participation
- Light volume pullback = weak sellers, buyers in control
- See: UTS_RISK_MANAGEMENT.md (volume calculations)

**Pillar 4: Float < 20M Shares**
- Aggressive target: <10M
- Very strong: <5M
- Low float + high volume = explosive moves
- See: UTS_OVERVIEW.md (float impact)

**Pillar 5: News Catalyst**
- Earnings beat, FDA approval, contract, short squeeze, sector rotation
- MUST understand WHY the stock is moving
- News often drops 8:00, 8:30, 9:00 AM EST
- See: UTS_ENTRY_RULES.md (catalyst confirmation)

## Entry Patterns (Ranked by Reliability)

1. **Bull Flag** - Flagpole (1-3 green candles) → Flag pullback (2-3 red on light volume) → Breakout
2. **Micro Pullback / First Candle to New High** - Stock trending up, pauses 1-2 candles, first green = entry
3. **ABCD Pattern** - A-to-B rally, B pullback, B-to-C leg up, C-to-D pullback (D must NOT break B low), entry on D breakout
4. **Dip Buy (3 Tricks)** - Light volume pullback + above 9 EMA + MACD open = buyable dip
5. **Flat Top Breakout** - Consolidation at resistance (2-3 touches same price) + volume spike = entry

## Stop Loss Rules (The 5 Rules for Selling Losers)

1. **Daily Max Loss** = Daily profit goal (hit it = done for day)
2. **Green to Red Rule** = Account turns red (profitable→losing) = STOP TRADING, walk away
3. **Give Back Half Rule** = Hit goal then give back >50% = walk away immediately
4. **Quality Stocks Only** = Trade ONLY all-5-pillars stocks (A+ setups), NEVER B-quality
5. **Trade Less** = 2-4 high-conviction trades > 10-15 mediocre ones

## Position Sizing Formula

```
Position Size = Max Loss Per Trade / Stop Loss Distance (in cents)

Example: Max loss = $200, Stop = 10 cents below entry
Shares = $200 / $0.10 = 2,000 shares
```

**Rule**: Risk 2-3% of account per trade

| Account | Risk/Trade | Daily Max Loss |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| $3,000 | $60-$90 | $200-$300 |
| $5,000 | $100-$150 | $300-$500 |
| $10,000 | $200-$300 | $500-$1,000 |

## Exit Strategy

**Minimum Risk/Reward**: 2:1 (if stop is 10¢, target must be 20¢+)

**Scaling Out** (do NOT sell all at once):
- Sell 50% at first profit target (typically 2:1 R/R or retest of high-of-day)
- Move stop to breakeven on remaining 50%
- Let remaining shares run toward 3:1 or next resistance

**Base Hit Targets** (Scalping): 15-25 cents/share
- Average winner: ~15 cents
- Average loser: ~10-12 cents
- On 1,000 shares = $150-$250 profit per trade

## Trading Psychology Rules

**Core Mental Rules**:
- Discipline Over Emotion: Follow rules even when uncomfortable
- No Revenge Trading: After loss, take 10-min break (or stop for day)
- Accept Losses as Tuition: Journal every loss, learn from it
- Track Every Trade: Entry/exit/P&L/pattern/time/emotional state = reveals behavior patterns
- Process Over Results: Good process on losing trade = GOOD trade. Bad process on winning trade = BAD trade
- Simulator First: Paper trade 2-4 weeks before real money

**Common Traps to Avoid**:
- FOMO (Fear of Missing Out): Buying at top because it moved 50%
- Anchoring to the High: "It was at $48, it'll come back" — NO, trade what's in front
- Overconfidence: After big win, increasing size = biggest losses happen
- Loss Aversion: Holding losers too long instead of cutting
- Revenge Trading: "I'll make it back on next trade" — this cascades losses

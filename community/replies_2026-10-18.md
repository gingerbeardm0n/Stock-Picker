## u/Partnikl (2026-10-15 21:28 UTC, reply to post)

If it was so simple! Premarket gappers are about execution, fills, timing and slipping most of the time. That's why bots, algos and other stuff have hard times to automate it. One second to late can mean your whole edge. Man, even half a second can make a difference of 10 cents, which is alread 100$ with 1000 Shares. And if your stop triggers, and you have big size on a gapper, that is not very liquid, you loose another 15 cents with slippage. If you automate a system and create an algo, you have to stresstest your fills. Best would be to look at the actual prints and compare it with your execution. If your entry hits at 3.40$, but it was a big order that soaked up every offer up to 3.48$, but you simulate your entry at 3.40$ because that was the trigger, your edge is already gone, and your stress fill is 3.48$ at best. And this is just one example out of many. It is extremely complex and if you want to automate it, you have to consider every little detail.
